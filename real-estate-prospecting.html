Prospecting support built around your market and workflow

Real estate prospecting requires consistent activity, a defined target audience, and a repeatable process. ZENTAKO supports agents and small teams with agreed homeowner outreach, prospect conversations, follow-up, qualification, and activity reporting.

What the service includes

  • Aligning on geographic areas and prospect criteria.
  • Working through client-approved lead lists.
  • Conducting professional outbound prospecting conversations through the client's calling setup.
  • Identifying and recording relevant selling interest.
  • Following up based on agreed rules and appropriate contact permissions.
  • Reporting activity and outcomes for review.

Who it may suit

Independent agents, small teams, and agents who have leads or prospect lists but need help with consistent follow-up and outreach. The service is designed to supplement the agent's work—not replace their market knowledge, client relationship, or listing presentation.

How engagements start

We agree on campaign scope, lead sources, target areas, qualification criteria, compliance requirements, calling tools, scheduling expectations, and reporting before outreach begins. The client supplies approved lead data and calling infrastructure for the initial model.

Frequently asked questions

What is real estate prospecting?

Real estate prospecting is the ongoing process of identifying and contacting potential clients, including homeowners who may consider selling, and developing conversations that can lead to business opportunities.

Does ZENTAKO provide lead lists?

For the initial service model, the client supplies the approved lead list and calling infrastructure. Lead sourcing arrangements can be discussed separately.

Can prospecting guarantee listings?

No listing or appointment results are guaranteed. Targets, criteria, workflow and reporting are agreed transparently before a campaign.
