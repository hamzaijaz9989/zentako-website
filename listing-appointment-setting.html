From homeowner conversations to scheduled appointments

Listing appointment setting focuses on identifying homeowners with relevant selling interest, understanding agreed qualification factors, and scheduling a conversation with the agent when the prospect meets the campaign criteria.

Qualification criteria are agreed in advance

Depending on the agent's market and campaign, criteria may include property location, homeowner or decision-maker status, expressed selling interest, timing, and willingness to schedule. Criteria are client-defined and must be used consistently.

Appointment workflow

  • Confirm target and qualification requirements.
  • Contact prospects using the agreed campaign process.
  • Clarify interest and timing respectfully.
  • Offer appointment slots based on the agent's calendar availability.
  • Confirm the appointment and pass notes to the agent.
  • Track appointment outcomes and feedback.

Clear roles

The client provides approved lead data, calling infrastructure, calendar availability, and feedback. ZENTAKO handles agreed prospecting, qualification, follow-up, appointment coordination, notes, and reporting.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a qualified listing appointment?

The client defines criteria before launch. Typical factors may include property or location fit, homeowner or decision-maker status, genuine selling interest, agreed timing, and a confirmed calendar booking.

Do you guarantee booked appointments?

No. ZENTAKO does not claim unverified guarantees. Campaign targets and reporting are discussed transparently.

Who controls the agent's calendar?

The client provides calendar availability and appointment preferences; scheduling follows those agreed instructions.
