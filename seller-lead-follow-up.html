Consistent follow-up for seller opportunities

Real estate leads may need several conversations before timing, interest, or readiness becomes clear. ZENTAKO helps agents follow up with homeowners according to an agreed workflow, capture useful context, and identify when a prospect is ready for a conversation with the agent.

Our follow-up workflow

  1. Review the client-approved lead and contact context.
  2. Follow the agreed communication cadence and permitted channels.
  3. Record responses, selling interest, timing, and next steps.
  4. Escalate relevant or ready prospects to the agent.
  5. Confirm scheduled appointments and share notes.

What we track

Depending on the campaign, reporting can include contact attempts, conversations, follow-up status, stated timing, qualification notes, appointment outcomes, and feedback. Reporting fields are agreed before campaign launch.

Why structured follow-up matters

A documented follow-up process helps teams avoid losing track of conversations, provides visibility into the pipeline, and supports timely responses. It does not replace the agent's judgment or create a guarantee of conversion.

Frequently asked questions

What is seller lead follow-up?

Seller lead follow-up is the process of contacting and reconnecting with homeowners who may have selling interest, recording relevant context, and arranging appropriate next steps.

How often are homeowners contacted?

The contact schedule is agreed with the client and must respect contact permissions, applicable laws, requests not to be contacted, and campaign rules.

What happens when a homeowner is interested?

Interest and context are recorded, then the prospect is handled according to agreed qualification criteria and appointment scheduling process.
