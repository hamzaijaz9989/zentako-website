Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations from day one.

Do you guarantee listing appointments?

We don't publish unverified guarantees. Before a campaign begins, we'll agree on the target, qualification criteria, workflow and reporting. Any performance targets will be discussed transparently.

Who supplies the leads and calling tools?

For our initial service model, the client supplies the approved lead list and calling infrastructure. This keeps the setup lean and allows us to focus on outreach and appointment-setting.

What makes an appointment qualified?

Qualification is defined with you before launch—typically including property/location fit, homeowner or decision-maker status, genuine selling interest, agreed timing and a confirmed calendar booking.

Which markets do you serve?

We work with real estate professionals in markets where campaign rules, data use and calling requirements can be reviewed and followed. Market and campaign details are confirmed before engagement.

How do we get started?

Start with a short conversation about your market, current lead sources, prospecting needs and the kind of appointments you want to book.
